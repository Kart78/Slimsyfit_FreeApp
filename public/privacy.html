Privacy · SlimSyFit FreeApp

This independent free wellness tracker stores information you choose to log, such as survey answers, meals, hydration, fasting, weight, sleep and activity.

Where data is stored

Guest data stays in the browser on your device. Signed-in accounts use the separate Supabase database configured for this FreeApp, and a local device copy. Google OAuth is handled through the app's configured Supabase project.

Purpose and access

Records are used to show your own wellness history and progress. Access to cloud records is restricted to the authenticated account through database row-level security. The app does not use Stripe, payment subscriptions, AI analysis or advertising SDKs.

Your controls

You can export your data or delete your tracking history from the app's settings. Complete account deletion requires the dedicated account-deletion function to be deployed. Deleting browser data may also remove guest records. Do not log information you don't want stored.

Third-party services

When configured, login and hosting are provided through Supabase, Google and Vercel under their respective terms. No data is migrated from the original SlimSyFit application automatically.
