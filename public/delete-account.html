Delete your FreeApp data or account

Sign in to the FreeApp, open the Me/settings screen, and choose Delete tracking history to delete health entries. To delete your login account as well, use Delete account and data when the separate Supabase account-deletion function is deployed.

For guests, delete tracking history in the app or clear the browser's local site data. There is no shared account or data link to the original SlimSyFit app.
